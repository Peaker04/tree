# NOVYX UI KIT — VISUAL REVIEW MATRIX

| Component / Area | Current visual quality | Status | Reason | Possible direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas & Tone** | Strong, warm, botanical base (`Honeydew` + `Cornsilk` variations). | **KEEP** | Creates a calm, outdoor-friendly atmosphere without defaulting to pure white or harsh gray. | Preserve as the definitive app atmosphere. |
| **Typography (Be Vietnam Pro)** | Highly legible, clean, handles diacritics well, good default scale. | **REFINE** | A bit generic. While highly readable, it lacks a distinct personality for headings and can feel "default modern". | Keep for body text. Consider a bolder/more distinct treatment for Hero numbers & Titles. |
| **PrimaryAction (CTA)** | Massive `Emerald` pills. Very functional but visually predictable. | **REFINE** | The pill shape combined with solid green can look like a consumer plant-watering app (toy-like) instead of a professional enterprise tool. | Shift geometry from 999px pill to a confident rounded rectangle (16-20px) to restore operational seriousness. |
| **Iconography** | Clean, rounded outline icons. | **REDESIGN** | Feels entirely like off-the-shelf "Material Symbols Rounded". It is functionally harmless but strips the app of a unique NOVYX identity. | Move to a slightly thicker, squircle-based or custom-feeling icon set (e.g., filled/duotone accents) to build brand DNA. |
| **Bottom Navigation** | Good structural return to 5-tabs, subtle active capsule. | **REFINE** | The centered QR action feels like it floats without strong enough integration. Active capsules can sometimes feel unanchored. | Tighten the visual housing for the QR FAB. Strengthen the active state contrast. |
| **Task / Evidence Surfaces** | Tonal surfaces instead of boxed cards. Good rhythm. | **KEEP** | Removing heavy drop-shadows and Card-in-Card nesting successfully killed the "ERP Admin Dashboard" feel. | Keep whitespace-first grouping. Ensure edge contrast is high enough for glare. |
| **Status / Pill Tokens** | Color-coded status pills are legible but repetitive. | **REFINE** | Having multiple shades of pale green/yellow (`Verveine`, `Willow Green`, `Limonade`) can look muddy or rainbow-like. | Limit status pills to fewer, tighter semantic colors. Introduce a stark neutral for non-critical states. |
